# Store Copy — OTP Buddy

## App Store (English)

**Name:** OTP Buddy  
**Subtitle:** Email codes to your clipboard  
**Keywords:** OTP,2FA,email,verification,IMAP,clipboard,menu bar  

**Description:**

OTP Buddy watches a mailbox you connect (IMAP) and surfaces one-time passcodes in the menu bar the moment they arrive.

Optionally auto-copy codes to the clipboard, or confirm with one click. Credentials live in the Keychain; codes are held briefly in memory and never uploaded.

**What's New:** Initial release.

**Privacy / Review notes:**

- App connects to user-provided IMAP host with user credentials (Keychain).
- Reads recent inbox messages locally to detect verification codes.
- Does not send email contents to our servers.
- Auto-copy is off by default.
